You Are Not Alone

Wherever you are, whatever you’re feeling, please know this: you matter. Your thoughts, your struggles, and your experiences are real. Reaching out for support is a sign of courage — and help is available. This page gathers trustworthy resources and ways to connect with people who care.





Take A Breath

It’s okay to feel overwhelmed. Life can be hard. But there is no shame in needing support. Whether you’re experiencing stress, anxiety, depression, or thoughts of self-harm, you are not weak — you are human. Support is out there.



Recognizing When To Reach Out

  • Persistent sadness, hopelessness, or numbness.
  • Feeling like you’re a burden or thinking about harming yourself.
  • Losing interest in things you used to enjoy.
  • Feeling overwhelmed by anxiety, panic, or intrusive thoughts.
  • Using substances more often to cope.

If any of this feels familiar, please consider reaching out to someone you trust or to one of the resources below.





Ways To Get Support

  • Talk to someone you trust: A friend, family member, mentor, teacher, or community leader can offer a listening ear.
  • Seek professional help: A counselor, therapist, or doctor can provide treatment and guidance.
  • Join a support group: Both online and in-person groups exist for anxiety, depression, grief, trauma, and more.
  • Use crisis lines or chat services: Immediate support is available, often 24/7, and free of charge.



Mental Health Support & Information

  • National Alliance on Mental Illness (NAMI)— Mental health information, support groups, and helplines (US-based).
  • Mental Health Foundation— Mental health resources and advice (UK-based but helpful globally).
  • Mind— Practical support and advice on mental health (UK).
  • Mental Health America— Screening tools, education, and support.



Free & Confidential Support Services

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US & Canada)— Call or text 988 (24/7, free, confidential).
  • Crisis Text Line— Text HELLO to 741741 (US), 686868 (Canada), or 85258 (UK) to connect with a trained listener.
  • Samaritans— Call 116 123 (UK & Ireland, 24/7).
  • International Suicide Hotlines Directory— Find crisis centers worldwide.



If You’re In Immediate Danger

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, please call your local emergency number (for example, 119 in Sri Lanka). Do not wait. Your safety is the most important thing.
